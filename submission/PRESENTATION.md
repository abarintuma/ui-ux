# Simple presentation and demonstration

## Before presenting

Open the [website](https://ui-ux-ivory.vercel.app/), the [Figma file](https://www.figma.com/design/FQLOMqCsZITyL52DR6MInm) and the [report](Budgetwise_Report.pdf). Start with a fresh browser workspace or restore sample data through Settings. Use October 2026 so the figures match the report.

## Three minute guide

**First 30 seconds:** “My project is Budgetwise, a personal budget management interface. It helps a user record income and expenses and see the remaining monthly balance. The proposed users include students and people managing their own income.”

**Next 30 seconds:** Show the three wireframes and the mobile Figma designs. Explain the clear labels, green colours, summary cards and Add transaction button. Say that the research is desk research and that participant feedback still needs to be collected.

**Next 90 seconds:**

1. Show Login. Submit with empty fields to demonstrate validation, then choose Explore the demo.
2. Show October income of 1,850,000 UGX, expenses of 982,000 UGX and balance of 868,000 UGX.
3. Choose Add transaction. Select Expense and enter “Transport to class”, amount 15000, date 24 October 2026 and category Transport.
4. Point out the preview balance of 853,000 UGX, then save.
5. Show the saved summary: expenses 997,000 UGX and balance 853,000 UGX.
6. Reload to show that the sample entry stays in this browser.

**Last 30 seconds:** “HTML provides the page structure, CSS controls the appearance and responsive layout, and JavaScript validates entries and calculates totals. This is a classroom prototype with simulated login and local storage. The next improvement is testing the interface with intended users.”

## Short answers

- **Why these colours?** They give the interface a consistent appearance and make main actions easy to identify.
- **How is balance calculated?** Total income minus total expenses for the selected month.
- **Is login secure?** It is simulated for the assignment, with public demo credentials.
- **Where is data kept?** In this browser's local storage; it is not synced to a server.
- **What user research was done?** Desk research using the brief and prototype walkthrough. No participant interviews are claimed.
